CardScan AI v3.4.0 — Excel contact manager export

In the Backup tab, tap Export to Excel (with Photos).
Creates an offline .xlsx workbook with:
- Dashboard of contacts, companies, cities and categories
- Filterable Contact Directory, including all contact fields and photo thumbnails
- Categories worksheet
- Card Photos gallery with all attached photos embedded
- Links from contact rows to the corresponding photo gallery row
- Frozen headers and alternating row colors

Photos are embedded directly into the Excel file; no separate folder is required.
The export is a snapshot; edits in Excel do not sync back to the app.
The Excel file may be large if you have many photos.
Export Backup (JSON) remains the restore/import method; Excel is for viewing/editing outside the app.
Based on v3.3.3; QR, OCR, photos, and duplicate detection retained.
